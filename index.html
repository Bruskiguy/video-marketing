Record your demos

Setup:

  • 📱 Any modern smartphone or webcam is fine.
  • 🎧 Record in a quiet room — clear audio is non-negotiable.
  • 🖼️ Frame yourself from the chest up, camera at eye level.
  • 📖 Copy each script into a teleprompter app.

Delivery:

  • Deliver each script exactly as written — treat “SUPERCOOL” as the SaaS product you're selling.
  • Multiple takes are fine — submit your best one, as a single continuous take.
  • No fancy editing needed — we're judging your delivery, not your editing skills.
3

Upload & submit

Name your files FirstName_LastName_Option#.mp4 (e.g., Jane_Doe_Option1.mp4).

Upload both videos to Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer — set permissions so anyone with the link can view.

Email your submission to caleb@diffdev.io with the subject line “Spokesperson Application — [Your Name]”, including:
