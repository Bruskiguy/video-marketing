Video Deck is a B2B video production company for SaaS and tech brands. We shoot, edit, and distribute high-performing videos for over 100 software companies — product demos, tutorials, video sales letters, and social content — using real human actors, not AI. Our clients pick their favorite spokespersons from our roster, and that roster is where you come in. We're looking for lively, expressive college students to join our on-demand talent pool as on-camera spokespeople. We're piloting the program in Kyle, Texas.

The gig

What you do: read scripts from a teleprompter, on camera, and deliver them with energy and personality. That's it — we handle scripting, editing, set design, and everything else.

How work comes in: this is an on-demand, performance-based role. Our SaaS clients place orders for a few videos at a time and request talent. Once you pass the assessment below, you're added to our board of available actors. When an order comes in that fits you, we book you.

Growth: do well and clients like you? You get requested by name — meaning more bookings, more often. Stick with one client or appear across many.

This is freelance gig work. There are no guaranteed minimum hours — how much you work depends entirely on demand and how well your videos land with clients.

Who we're looking for

  • You're 18 or older (required).
  • You're fun, bubbly, and full of character — lively, expressive, comfortable being yourself on camera. Think "the friend everyone wants in their group project."
  • You can read from a teleprompter and make it sound natural — not like you're reading. This is the #1 skill.
  • You're a clear English speaker with neutral or easily understandable delivery.
  • You're comfortable in front of a camera — no prior acting experience needed; confidence and personality matter more than a résumé.
  • We celebrate diversity — applicants from all backgrounds and communities are encouraged to apply.

How to apply

Step 1 — Pick 2 of the 3 scripts below

Choose the two that best show off your range:

  • Option 1 — Corporate & Professional: tests B2B authority, clear articulation, executive presence.
  • Option 2 — Ultra-Casual & Irreverent: tests deadpan delivery, comedic timing, natural authenticity.
  • Option 3 — High-Energy D2C / Direct Challenge: tests rapid-fire cadence, screen presence, persuasive urgency.

💡 Tip: picking two contrasting styles (e.g., Option 1 + Option 2) shows us more range than two similar ones.

Step 2 — Record your demos

Setup:

  • 📱 Any modern smartphone or webcam is fine.
  • 🎧 Record in a quiet room. Clear audio is non-negotiable.
  • 🖼️ Frame yourself from the chest up, camera at eye level.
  • 📖 Copy and paste the script into your choice of a teleprompter app.

Delivery:

  • Deliver each script exactly as written — these are real client-style scripts, so treat "SUPERCOOL" as the SaaS product you're selling.
  • Multiple takes are fine — submit your best one. No fancy editing needed — a single raw take is perfect. We're judging your script delivery, not your editing skills.
  • Each demo should be one continuous take per script.

Step 3 — Upload and submit

  • Name your files FirstName_LastName_Option#.mp4 (e.g., Jane_Doe_Option1.mp4).
  • Upload both videos to Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer and share the link — make sure permissions are set so anyone with the link can view.
  • Send your submission to caleb@diffdev.io with the subject line: "Spokesperson Application — [Your Name]"
  • Please include in your email: your name and university/major; confirmation that you're 18+; the link to your two videos; and (optional) a link to any social media, TikTok, or past video work.

What happens next

  1. Assessment review — we review your two demo recordings.
  2. Pass? You're on the board. Successful applicants are added to our board of available actors.
  3. Orders come in. Our SaaS clients place orders for a few videos at a time and request talent. When you match what they need, we reach out to book you.
  4. You get paid. $100 per script, delivered in 15–30 minutes of recording time.
  5. Rise or rest. If you do well and the client loves you, you'll get requested again and again. Your workload grows with your performance.
